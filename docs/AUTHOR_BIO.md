# Author Biography

Shanmukh Behara is an advisor in data science and analytics, currently Data Governance Advisor at
Southern California Edison, where he builds the pipelines, validation frameworks and reporting
that decide whether a number can be trusted before anyone acts on it.

He has spent more than eight years in the field. He began as a risk analyst at Uber,
writing SQL-based fraud detection views and classification models that flagged risky driver and
customer transactions. At Swiggy he was a senior business strategy analyst, building demand
forecasting models and leading a team of ten across twelve cities as the operation scaled past
150,000 orders a day. Since 2023 he has been at Southern California Edison, first as a data
analyst — automating usage and generation pipelines, and designing the Power BI dashboards that
manage more than $1B in power contracts — and now in data governance, where his work includes a
Python integration pipeline rebuilt as an Azure-native service with managed secrets and a
28-test automated suite, twenty-two validation checks spanning SAP, Azure and Power BI, and the
metadata and business-glossary work that gives a shared term a single agreed definition.

He holds an MS in Business Analytics and Operations Research from California State University
(GPA 3.8, Dean's List). He works in Python, R and SQL, across SAP, Azure and the AWS data stack,
with Power BI and Tableau for reporting.

*From Absolute Zero* is built on one discipline: every program in the book is executed and its
real output printed on the page, including results that undercut the chapter they appear in — a
convolutional network that scores below the simpler model preceding it, a transformer that loses
to a simpler predecessor. The book treats those outcomes as worth keeping rather than tidying
away.
