# M04 · Machine learning breadth screen

45 minutes · round: breadth screen · suits: data scientist, machine learning engineer

**How to run it.** Set a timer for the full time. Answer each question aloud, in order, and keep to its minutes; record yourself if you can. For a coding item, open the exercise and write the solution without looking at its solution file. Do not open the review sheet until the time is up.

| # | Question | Minutes |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | **Q42** What is a baseline model and why does it matter? | 3 |
| 2 | **Q44** Give three concrete examples of data leakage. | 5 |
| 3 | **Q45** Why cross-validate instead of using a single train-test split? | 3 |
| 4 | **Q48** Explain the bias-variance tradeoff. | 4 |
| 5 | **Q51** Your model has 99% training accuracy and 71% validation accuracy. What do you do? | 5 |
| 6 | **Q55** Two of your features are highly correlated. What happens and what do you do? | 4 |
| 7 | **Q64** What is the difference between a random forest and gradient boosting? | 4 |
| 8 | **Q88** Which models require feature scaling and which do not? | 3 |
| 9 | **Q92** You tried 200 configurations and the best validation accuracy is 0.91. What do you report? | 5 |
| 10 | **Q96** Your model has AUC 0.92 but is useless in production. What could explain that? | 5 |
| 11 | **Q98** Where should you set the decision threshold? | 4 |

## After the timer

Open the [review sheet](m04-machine-learning-breadth-screen-review.md) and compare each answer with its note. Then ask:

- Did I state my assumptions before answering?
- Did I name the trade-off instead of claiming one right answer?
- Did I say what I would check, and what would change my mind?
- What would the follow-up question be, and could I answer it?
- Where I stalled, which chapter does the question point to?
