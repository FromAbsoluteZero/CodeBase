# M01 · Analyst breadth screen

45 minutes · round: breadth screen · suits: analyst, analytics engineer

**How to run it.** Set a timer for the full time. Answer each question aloud, in order, and keep to its minutes; record yourself if you can. For a coding item, open the exercise and write the solution without looking at its solution file. Do not open the review sheet until the time is up.

| # | Question | Minutes |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | **Q6** How would you check a file before analysing it? | 4 |
| 2 | **Q7** What is the grain of a table, and why does it matter? | 4 |
| 3 | **Q9** Explain the difference between INNER, LEFT, and FULL OUTER JOIN. | 4 |
| 4 | **Q14** WHERE or HAVING — how do you decide? | 3 |
| 5 | **Q16** Why does WHERE x = NULL return nothing? | 3 |
| 6 | **Q33** When does the mean mislead, and what do you report instead? | 4 |
| 7 | **Q32** A test returns p = 0.12. Your manager concludes the two options are identical. Respond. | 5 |
| 8 | **Q101** Why is 88% accuracy a bad result at a 12% base rate? | 4 |
| 9 | **Q174** Revenue is down 8%. How do you investigate? | 8 |
| 10 | **Q173** A stakeholder wants a dashboard showing everything. What do you ask? | 6 |

## After the timer

Open the [review sheet](m01-analyst-breadth-screen-review.md) and compare each answer with its note. Then ask:

- Did I state my assumptions before answering?
- Did I name the trade-off instead of claiming one right answer?
- Did I say what I would check, and what would change my mind?
- What would the follow-up question be, and could I answer it?
- Where I stalled, which chapter does the question point to?
