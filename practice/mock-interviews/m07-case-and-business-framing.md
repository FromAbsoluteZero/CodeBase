# M07 · Case and business framing

45 minutes · round: design or case · suits: analyst, data scientist, product

**How to run it.** Set a timer for the full time. Answer each question aloud, in order, and keep to its minutes; record yourself if you can. For a coding item, open the exercise and write the solution without looking at its solution file. Do not open the review sheet until the time is up.

| # | Question | Minutes |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | **Q168** A stakeholder wants to add AI to a workflow. How do you evaluate whether it is worth doing? | 8 |
| 2 | **Q169** Your model is accurate but adoption is low. What do you investigate? | 7 |
| 3 | **Q170** How would you measure the business value of a deployed model? | 7 |
| 4 | **Q174** Revenue is down 8%. How do you investigate? | 8 |
| 5 | **Q176** How do you handle a request for a number by end of day when the data is messy? | 5 |
| 6 | **Q178** What would make you refuse to run an analysis? | 5 |
| 7 | **Q183** You are given a vague request with no clear success metric. What do you do? | 5 |

## After the timer

Open the [review sheet](m07-case-and-business-framing-review.md) and compare each answer with its note. Then ask:

- Did I state my assumptions before answering?
- Did I name the trade-off instead of claiming one right answer?
- Did I say what I would check, and what would change my mind?
- What would the follow-up question be, and could I answer it?
- Where I stalled, which chapter does the question point to?
